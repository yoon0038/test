Welcome to the World of Dolphins!

Fun Dolphin Facts:

  • The name "dolphin" can be interpreted as "a 'fish' with a womb", from the Greek words delphis (dolphin) and delphys (womb).
  • Dolphins are very social and can form superpods of over 1,000 individuals when food is plentiful!
  • Dolphins sleep with one half of their brain at a time, allowing them to continue breathing and stay alert.
  • Some dolphins, like the Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphin, use tools! They teach their young to use sponges to protect their snouts while searching for food.
  • Bottlenose dolphins have unique "signature whistles", much like names, and can remember the whistles of other dolphins for more than 20 years.
